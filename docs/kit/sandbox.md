# Sandbox

The demo deployment carries two showcase shops built from synthetic data:

| Shop | Slug | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Elite Unisex Salon & Academy (sample menu) | `elite` | two branches, services, stylists, live slots, packages |
| Prabhave (sample catalogue) | `prabhave` | pieces and stitched suits, an offer with a code, orders at each step |

Their reads are open: point any example on these pages at the demo's host. The examples in the reference were
recorded from these two shops.

Requests you send on the demo are kept only as sample values. Payments are never processed and messages are never sent.
