What the kit can do

  • Show the service menu and prices, per branch
  • Show packages and published offers
  • Take a booking request, naming the branch

Every price the website shows is a row in your books. Nothing the kit sends changes those rows until you approve it.

Customers are made up and prices are samples until the shop's real menu arrives (the kirana is wholly synthetic). Sends go only to consenting test phones; payments are recorded, never processed. Every figure carries the stored row it was read from, or says "Cannot verify".
