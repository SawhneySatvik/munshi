What the kit can do

  • Show your catalogue and prices
  • Show published offers
  • Take a booking request or an enquiry

Every price the website shows is a row in your books. Nothing the kit sends changes those rows until you approve it.

Demo on synthetic data. Customers are made up; sends go only to consenting test phones; payments are recorded, never processed. Every figure carries the stored row it was read from, or says "Cannot verify".
