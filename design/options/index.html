Munshi design options

MS-16d · Wed 28 Oct 2026 · nothing gets built until you pick

Four things to decide, four options each. Every preview is live HTML, shown at desktop width (scaled to fit) and at phone width beside it. Tap the dotted figures, press Approve, try the colour swatches and the service tabs. Pick one per section and send the ids back, for example site-A · app-B · elite-C · suit-A.

  • Munshi's own pages use the decided "calm ledger" feel in deep indigo and saffron (D-042). One website option is an honest alternative palette and says so.
  • Elite and the suit shop are real shops. Their prices and slots are samples, and the drawn art stands in for their photos. The kirana in the app is synthetic, and its supplier is simulated.
  • All four website sections keep the storefront family you liked (D-040). Each option changes the palette, type, detail and motion.

1Munshi's product website

Hero with “Log in” and “Sign up” in the header, one feature row (tap a figure to see its record) and a “How it starts” strip. No prices are shown because none are decided. The dotted figures open their source records.

Recommendation: site-A: it is the decided calm-ledger look, and its hero proves the promise (tap a figure, see the bill) rather than describing it. site-C is the most distinctive if you want Hindi to lead.
