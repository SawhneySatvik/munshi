Why round 1 failed

All four round-1 shop options were one page in four costumes: a text-left hero beside a cartoon kurta on a hanger, a scrolling offer band, four pastel tiles with clip-art icons, and a product box, with only the colours and fonts swapped. The drawings did most of the damage. Thick 2.4 px outlines and flat primary fills read as a children's app. The festive props (marigold garlands, diyas, fireworks, bandhani dots) turned a year-round suit shop into a Diwali flyer, and the palettes copied occasions (wedding-card maroon, haldi yellow) instead of cloth. Nothing on the page looked like fabric: no drape, no weave, no border. And no frame was cut for the owner's real photographs. Round 2 drops every prop. It draws cloth instead of costumes, as tonal fabric plates and fine technical flats in frames cut to real photo ratios, and it changes the structure from option to option, not just the colours.

Spelling. प्रभावे is our proposed Hindi rendering of Prabhave. Please check it with the owner; the shop's own signboard spelling wins.

Placeholders. Every drawing sits in a 4:5 or 3:2 photo slot. When the owner's photos arrive, each one drops into its slot with no layout change. The art is drawn fresh as inline SVG; there are no photos, generated images or stock art.

Facts. Every price, count, hour and offer code is a sample and is labelled. No address, phone number, WhatsApp number or owner name is invented. Each hold says plainly that nothing is set aside until the owner says yes.

Prabhave: six directions

Each option shows the same seven things: a header with the wordmark, हिंदी and Ask on WhatsApp; a hero; shop by kind (unstitched, semi-stitched, stitched, dupattas); a product with colour and size; Request a hold; an offer with a code; and Visit us · Kirti Nagar. What changes is the structure, the palette and the type. Three options are light, two are dark, and one is a mid-grey studio.

Recommendation: prabhave-D, Come by Kirti Nagar. It is built around how this shop actually sells: ask on WhatsApp, the owner approves a hold, the customer comes in. It looks premium without leaning on photography, because the hero is the shop itself. Its marble, velvet and brass sit close to Elite's restraint without copying it, and the owner's phone photos will hold up inside it. Runner-up: prabhave-A, Cover story. It is the most fashion-led of the six and the closest to the taste you showed with Elite, but it needs good photographs to stay that way.

How the light mode was tuned

  • Gold champagne #D9C49C is only 1.6:1 on porcelain, so text gold deepens to antique gold #80632A (5.1:1). Champagne survives as the lettering on black buttons.
  • Buttons and tabs black pills with champagne type: dark mode's gold-on-black, inverted.
  • Line weight the mirror's stroke goes from 1.6 to 1.8 px in a warmer gold, because thin gold lines fade on a light ground.
  • Mirror glass a faint champagne wash fills the arch, with a white gleam: on light, the mirror reads by reflection, not by glow.
  • Depth no shadows on black; on porcelain the branch cards take one soft, warm shadow so they do not dissolve into the page.
  • Toggle a sun or moon button in the header, 44 px, beside Book a visit; the site can open in either mode or follow the phone.
