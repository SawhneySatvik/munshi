Round 1 showed one layout in four coats of paint. These six differ where it matters: how the home is organised, the palette, and the type. Each one is the owner’s phone home and the same screen on a laptop, and each one works. Tap an underlined figure to open its record, approve or reject what needs you, switch EN/हिं, or press the mic.

The same in all six

  • Every figure traces. A faint dotted underline marks a figure read from the books, and tapping it opens the record behind it. A figure that cannot be traced reads “Cannot verify” rather than a guess.
  • Nothing moves without a person. Munshi’s drafts wait under “Needs you” with Approve and Reject. Each decision leaves a receipt that names who made it.
  • Voice is one tap away. The mic sits in the bottom navigation. In this mock it answers one fixed question, with its source.
  • The shop’s brand fits. Four tokens (chrome, text on chrome, brand, text on brand) re-theme the frame and the main action. Content colours and states stay the system’s, so contrast holds whatever the brand is.
  • Labelled honestly. The shop is synthetic, the supplier is simulated, and payments are recorded, never processed.
